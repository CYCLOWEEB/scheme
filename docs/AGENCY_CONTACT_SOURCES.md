# Agency phone and office-hour sources

Research checked: 2026-10-15.

The website displays 32 government-published phone contacts and three published office schedules. Each field links to its own source. Existing agency names, addresses and map positions are unchanged.

Only government-hosted sources were used. A contact must match the existing agency and office; related agencies, personal mobile numbers and fax-only lines were excluded. Some official sources designate a combined telephone/fax number. Numbers were checked against published sources and for complete Indian STD format; no live test calls were made. Some government pages were available through the search index while direct requests timed out.

Office schedules are presented as approximate visiting guidance in Indian Standard Time. No live open/closed status, inferred weekday schedules, general department hours or third-party business-directory hours were added. The existing request to call first still applies.
